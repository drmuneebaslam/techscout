    - Article Submission & Editor System: Added prominent Word (.docx) manuscript preference notice across author studio and dashboard; enhanced visual editor with live word counter, character counter, and reading time estimator in ribbon toolbar.
    - Sponsored Systems & Crypto Payments: Implemented public /sponsor portal for tools and articles with admin-configured crypto wallet addresses (BTC, ETH, USDT, SOL) and transaction hash (TXID) verification queue in admin.
    - Speed Optimization: Configured mod_deflate Gzip compression, mod_expires, and Cache-Control headers in .htaccess.
    - Admin UI/UX 100x Polish: Enhanced assets/css/admin.css with glassmorphism, micro-glow badges, hover lift states, and dark/light mode preservation without inline styles.
