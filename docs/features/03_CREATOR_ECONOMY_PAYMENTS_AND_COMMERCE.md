# 💳 Module 3: 50+ Creator Economy, Payments & Commerce (Features 101–150)

This module outlines 50+ pure software monetization capabilities, payment gateways, multi-rail disbursals, revenue splits, digital asset commerce, and financial ledgers.

---

## 💰 1. Partner Monetization & Royalty Mechanics (Features 101–115)

101. **Multi-Rail Instant Payout Disbursals**: Support for Stripe Connect Express, PayPal Payouts API, Bank Wire (ACH/SEPA/SWIFT), and Crypto (USDT / USDC on Arbitrum & Polygon).
102. **Dynamic Co-Author Smart Splits**: Contract framework allowing multiple contributors to automatically split article royalties and tips according to defined percentage shares.
103. **Per-Second Active Attention Royalty Engine**: Calculates creator payouts based on validated active reading time (focus time, scroll velocity, code copying) rather than shallow page views.
104. **Automated Tax Compliance (W-8BEN / W-9) Onboarding**: Embedded digital tax intake collecting valid IRS tax classification, TIN/SSN/EIN, and calculating treaty withholding rates.
105. **Direct Reader Tipping Jar**: Embedded tipping widget on articles and author profiles supporting preset ($1, $5, $25) and custom fiat or crypto tip amounts.
106. **Tiered Paid Subscriber Memberships**: Authors can launch monthly/annual subscription plans (e.g. *$5/mo Insider Pass*) with exclusive technical deep dives.
107. **Author Payout Threshold & Auto-Schedule Controls**: Creators can configure custom withdrawal thresholds (from $20 to $1,000) and choose weekly or monthly payout cycles.
108. **Real-Time Earnings Ticker & Run-Rate Forecaster**: Author studio widget showing live accrued royalties second-by-second with 30-day forecast projections.
109. **Multi-Currency Real-Time FX Conversion**: Displays author earnings and reader pricing dynamically in USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, and JPY.
110. **Promo Credits to Cash Payout Converter**: Monetized authors can convert earned promotional advertising credits into direct cash disbursals.
111. **Author Milestone Bonus Pool**: Automated bonus payouts disbursed upon reaching verified milestones (e.g. *$250 bonus for 25k monthly qualified reads*).
112. **Monthly Payout Audit & Disbursal Ledger**: Detailed admin ledger recording gross claim, processing fees, net disbursal, payment method, and transaction reference codes.
113. **Disbursal Failure Auto-Recovery & SMS Alerts**: Automated retry engine and instant SMS/email alerts if a creator's bank or PayPal rejects a payment.
114. **Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) Pricing Adjustments**: Subscription and tip prices automatically adjust based on the reader's geographic location to maximize global accessibility.
115. **Early Payout Advance for Verified Master Legends**: High-reputation creators can request early withdrawal of accrued royalties prior to the monthly settlement date.
